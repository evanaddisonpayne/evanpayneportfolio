# French (fr-FR) translation notes

For Evan. Files: `content/fr.ts`, `content/cases/fr.ts`, `content/articles/you-all-sound-the-same.fr.tsx`, `content/map/fr.json`.

## Register

- **Vous** throughout, as briefed. I kept the edge with short sentences, spoken syntax ("Parlons-en", "Alors, on fait quoi ?", "Ça coûte des millions") and the dry asides. I'd stay with vous. A Paris CMO reading a senior strategist's site expects vous, and *tu* would read as startup-bro.
- The **site pages** stay first person, as in English. The **resume** uses French CV convention for bullets: nominal phrases ("Pilotage de…", "Supervision de…", "Refonte de…"). This is how a French director's CV reads, and it keeps the credit verbs exact (led → direction / à la tête de, oversaw → supervision, ran → pilotage). "Helped make an Emmy-nominated TV show" stays first person ("J'ai aidé à fabriquer…") so the "helped" can't get lost in a nominal phrase.
- **Titles stay in English** (Director of Brand & Growth Strategy, Chief of Staff, Marketing Strategist, etc.). A French recruiter reads them as is, and translating them ("Directeur de la stratégie de marque…") would change how the job reads against LinkedIn.

## Terminology kept in English

- **Work Tech** and **HR tech**: kept as in English, same casing. French B2B marketers say "la HR tech" (also "RH tech" or "la tech RH", but the agency world uses the English). Work Tech has no French equivalent in use. Treated as feminine ("la Work Tech").
- Kept: B2B, SaaS, SEO, NPS, ARR, MQL, SQL, CAC, KPI, ABM, AEO, go-to-market, pipeline, upsell, cross-sell, demand gen, paid media, thought leadership, deck, pitch deck, funnel, full-funnel, branding, rebranding, tagline, shortlist, win rate, design sprints, analytics, framework, social media, playlist. Each is what a Paris agency would actually say.
- Translated where French is natural: "messaging" as a deliverable → *messages*; "messaging workshop" stays **atelier messaging** (agency usage); positioning → *positionnement*; brand → *marque*; revenue → *chiffre d'affaires* (but "recurring annual revenue" → *revenus annuels récurrents*); enterprise (clients) → *grands comptes*; key accounts → *comptes clés*; PR → *RP*; CSR → *RSE*.
- **Money**: one convention everywhere, `M$` and `k$` with a no-break space: « 1,72 M$ », « 10 M$ », « 125 k$ », « +600 k$ ». Decimal comma, « 78 % », « 1 000 ».
- **Newsletter**: the eyebrow reads "La newsletter · Sur LinkedIn, en anglais". That is the one mention that Brand Therapy is in English (still two segments, so the separator split is unchanged).
